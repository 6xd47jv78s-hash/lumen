4.1.1.1 | Cell structure | Describe the sub-cellular structures of eukaryotic cells (animal and plant) and prokaryotic cells, and state the differences between them.
4.1.1.2 | Cell structure | Explain how the main sub-cellular structures (nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, mitochondria, ribosomes; plus chloroplasts, cell wall and permanent vacuole in plants) are related to their functions.
4.1.1.3 | Cell structure | Explain how the size and scale of cells can be calculated, and use and convert between standard units (milli-, micro-, nano-).
4.1.1.4 | Cell structure | Explain how electron microscopy has increased understanding of sub-cellular structures compared with light microscopy, including magnification and resolving power.
4.1.2.1 | Cell division | Describe the cell cycle, including the role of mitosis in growth, repair and asexual reproduction.
4.1.2.2 | Cell division | Describe the function of stem cells in embryos, adult animals and plant meristems, and discuss issues around the use of stem cells in medicine.
4.1.3.1 | Transport in cells | Explain how substances move in and out of cells by diffusion, and the factors that affect the rate of diffusion.
4.1.3.2 | Transport in cells | Explain osmosis as the diffusion of water across a partially permeable membrane, and describe the effect of solutions of different concentrations on plant and animal cells.
4.1.3.3 | Transport in cells | Describe active transport as the movement of substances against a concentration gradient, requiring energy from respiration, with examples.
